# Thai flashcard: uiverse.io component shortlist

`components.html` renders all 16 shortlisted components restyled to the app palette, at phone width.
All are MIT licensed (uiverse.io); author + link are kept in a comment above each block.

Source: uiverse.io itself was blocked by this environment's network policy, so components were
pulled from uiverse's official mirror repo (github.com/uiverse-io/galaxy, same components, same
authors) and judged visually by rendering ~400 candidates in headless Chromium, idle and tapped.

## Top pick per job

| Job | Top pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| a. Answer buttons | [KhelVers/orange-lionfish-62](https://uiverse.io/KhelVers/orange-lionfish-62) | A solid ledge that collapses on tap is the most physical press with zero gradients, and it takes a correct/wrong colour swap cleanly. |
| b. Flip card | [joe-watson-sbf/little-goat-24](https://uiverse.io/joe-watson-sbf/little-goat-24) | The dog-eared corner makes it read as a paper flashcard, which fits the warm-paper look. |
| c. Feedback | [mrpumps31232/heavy-bear-50](https://uiverse.io/mrpumps31232/heavy-bear-50) (correct) + [gharsh11032000/cuddly-squid-93](https://uiverse.io/gharsh11032000/cuddly-squid-93) (wrong) | One draws a check, the other shakes and fills red: shortlisted as a pair because they cover the two outcomes. |
| d. Word popover | [Quezaquo/bitter-wolverine-27](https://uiverse.io/Quezaquo/bitter-wolverine-27) | The paper-coloured bubble stays quiet over Thai text, and the springy pop confirms the tap without stealing focus. |
| e. Background | [artvelog/splendid-quail-83](https://uiverse.io/artvelog/splendid-quail-83) | Ruled lines with an ink-red margin make the Read mode feel like a marked-up page. |
| f. Loader | [vinodjangid07/good-cow-5](https://uiverse.io/vinodjangid07/good-cow-5) | A book flipping its pages tells the learner a story is being written, which 5-10 s needs more than a spinner. |
| g. Theme toggle | [JaydipPrajapati1910/splendid-panda-57](https://uiverse.io/JaydipPrajapati1910/splendid-panda-57) | The labelled halves are 44px tall and say what they do, where an icon-only switch has to be guessed. |
| h. Progress | [Juanes200122/light-seahorse-74](https://uiverse.io/Juanes200122/light-seahorse-74) | The count bubble riding the bar ("7/10") gives progress and the number in one glance. |

e2 BadlyWrittenStylesheet, f2 vk-uiux (skeleton), g1 DawidMazurek4, h2 dylanharriscameron (combo meter).
